Plan your Music experience

A little preparation helps everyone focus on the music rather than the logistics and ensures a great experience is had by all.

Planning Tips

01

Check the essentials

Confirm the date, doors time, performance time, age policy and whether tickets are seated or standing.

02

Plan the journey

Allow for queues and city-centre traffic. Identify the final public transport service and agree a backup route.

03

Review accessibility

Check step-free routes, accessible toilets, seating options, companion policies and hearing support before booking.

04

Protect your hearing

For louder shows, bring properly fitted reusable earplugs and take short breaks away from speakers.

05

Look after your group

Choose a meeting point, keep phones charged and make sure everyone knows how they are getting home.

06

Respect the performance

Follow the venue's camera policy, keep screens dim and give performers and audience members space. Most importantly, enjoy the music!
