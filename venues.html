Venue Guide

Different spaces create different experiences. Use this guide to choose the setting that suits you and your group.

Which space suits your night of music?

The guidelines below are broad planning insights for venue types, not listings for individual businesses or venues. Always confirm exact details, age restrictions and accessibility with the venue.

Venue type Typical audience Atmosphere Often suits Plan for
Pub session room 30 to 120 Informal, social Traditional, acoustic Limited seating and conversation nearby
Club or small stage 100 to 500 Close, energetic Indie, electronic, jazz Standing space and later finish
Festival or large stage 1000 to 5000 Vibrant, energetic Contemporary, music artists, pop Standing space , possibly outdoor and later finish
Theatre 400 to 1,500 Focused, comfortable Singer-songwriters, comedy music, touring acts Allocated seating and interval timing
Concert hall 800 to 2,000+ Formal, acoustically detailed Orchestral, choral, contemporary ensemble Programme timing and quieter etiquette
