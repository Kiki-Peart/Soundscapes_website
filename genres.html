Getting into the scene

Hear your sound. Find your soul

Dublin's music culture spans a wide array of venues, from intimate acoustic rooms, energetic stages to formal and informal concert settings.

Traditional Irish music

Sessions are social and spontaneous. Players trade tunes, build momentum and respond to the room. Listen respectfully, keep conversation low near performers, and allow the natural rhythm of the session to lead the evening.

Best for: storytelling, shared atmosphere and close-up musicianship.

Indie and alternative

Small and mid-sized stages provide space for emerging artists, guitar bands and experimental acts. These shows often reward curiosity, with support slots offering a chance to hear new performers first.

Best for: discovery, lively crowds and new ideas.

Jazz and soul

Expect expressive vocals, improvisation and close communication between musicians. A smaller room can make subtle changes in tempo and mood feel especially immediate.

Best for: attentive listening and relaxed evenings.

Classical and contemporary

Orchestras, ensembles, soloists and new composers bring precision and contrast to the stage. Check the programme beforehand, but do not worry about being an expert. Curiosity is enough.

Best for: detail, drama and focused listening.
